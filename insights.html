Insights

Our purpose with Insights is to help organizations learn from real-world events and apply those lessons to their own operations. We examine major industrial accidents, disasters, regulatory issues, emergency situations, and safety challenges to understand what happened, why it happened, and what leaders and organizations can learn from those experiences. We believe that effective safety is more than policies and compliance—it requires strong leadership, communication, accountability, and practical systems. Through our articles, we explore how organizations can recognize hazards, respond to warning signs, strengthen critical controls, and create cultures where employees and leaders take ownership of safety. Our goal is to turn lessons from the past into safer decisions for the future.
